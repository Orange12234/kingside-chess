      Kingside rules

      Short version: play fair, be kind, and play as yourself. Moderators and the owner enforce these rules, and a real person looks at every automatic flag before anything happens.

      1. Fair play

      • No engines or computer help. Do not use Stockfish, another chess engine, an analysis board or any bot to choose your moves in a game against a person. The Play bots page is for playing the computer, not for getting help in rated games.
      • No outside help. No asking another person, a coach or a stream chat for moves while your game is going. No opening books or tablebases during a game.
      • No playing through someone else. Do not let another person play your account, and do not play on someone else's.
      • The system checks. After each rated game the server looks at how accurate each side played. A player whose recent rated games show both a very high win rate and very high accuracy is flagged for the moderators and the owner. A flag is not a punishment: a moderator looks at the games first, and a strong honest player can be flagged too.

      2. Ratings

      • No rating manipulation. Do not lose on purpose, trade wins with a friend, or use a second account to push a rating up or down.
      • No sandbagging. Do not play badly on purpose to lower your rating and then beat weaker players.
      • Rating changes get smaller the higher your rating is, and every category (bullet, blitz, rapid) has its own rating. Ratings are decided by the server only.
      • If an account is banned for good, the rating points it took from other players are given back to them.

      3. Aborting and leaving games

      • You may abort a game before you have made your first move. Aborted games are not rated and change nobody's rating.
      • Aborting a lot is reported: after 3 aborts in 24 hours you get a warning, and at 5 the moderators and the owner are told. It is a flag for a human to look at, nothing happens automatically.
      • Do not walk away from games you are losing and do not stall on purpose. If you run out of time you lose on time, as usual.

      4. Behaviour and chat

      • Be respectful. No insults, harassment, threats, hate speech or discrimination, in chat, in usernames or on profiles.
      • No spam, advertising or scams, and no sharing other people's private details.
      • Do not spam friend requests or challenges. Players can say no, and you can remove a friend at any time. Repeatedly pestering someone can be reported like any other harassment.
      • Usernames must not be offensive, must not pretend to be staff or a titled player, and must not copy someone else's name to fool people. Titles (GM, IM and so on) are only given by the owner.

      5. Accounts

      • An email address is needed to sign up. It is private. It is not checked, but it is used to link accounts: if one email has several accounts and one of them is banned, all of them are banned, for the same reason.
      • A banned email cannot be used to sign up again. Making new accounts to get around a ban is ban evasion, and those accounts will be banned too.
      • You are responsible for what happens on your account. Keep your password to yourself.

      6. Reporting

      • Use the Report player button on a player's profile and say what happened. Moderators see your username and your message.
      • Report honestly. Do not send false or joke reports, and do not report the same player again and again. You can send up to 5 reports a day.

      7. Tournaments

      • Only the owner starts tournaments. Games are rated, and every rule above applies. Cheating in a tournament is treated as cheating anywhere else.
      • When you join, you are paired automatically for as long as it runs. If you have to leave, press Pause so your opponents are not left waiting. A game you never move in is aborted, and you are paused automatically.
      • Do not team up with a friend to feed each other points, and do not lose on purpose. The standings are checked, and moderators can remove players from a tournament result by banning them.

      8. What happens if you break the rules

      • Moderators and the owner can ban an account for a set time or for good, always with a written reason you can see when you try to log in.
      • Cheating, rating manipulation and ban evasion are the most serious and can lead to a permanent ban of every account linked to the same email.
      • Appeals: if you think a ban was a mistake, try to log in. The login box shows your ban and an Appeal this ban button. Write what happened (you need your password to prove the account is yours). One appeal at a time, and a moderator reads every one.

      These rules can change. Playing on Kingside means you agree to them.
